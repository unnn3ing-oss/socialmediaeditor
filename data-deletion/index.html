資料刪除說明

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

「小編工具箱」不提供一般大眾以臉書帳號登入，因此不保存一般臉書使用者的個人資料。本工具保存哪些資料、保存多久，請見隱私權政策第 3 節。

1. 撤銷本工具的臉書授權

授權過本工具的臉書帳號，可以隨時在臉書移除授權：

  1. 登入臉書，點右上角大頭貼 →「設定和隱私」→「設定」。
  2. 進入「應用程式和網站」（或「商業整合」）。
  3. 找到本工具，按「移除」。

移除後，本工具保存的存取權杖會立即失效，無法再代表您或您的粉專發文。

2. 要求刪除本工具保存的資料

如需刪除本工具保存的存取權杖、粉專設定、留言範本或待發留言，請寄信至 jeanzhuang@tvbs.com.tw，主旨寫「小編工具箱 資料刪除申請」，並附上臉書帳號名稱或粉專名稱。我們會在收到後 30 天內刪除並回信確認。

3. 團隊成員的授權與資料

團隊成員的排程發布權限由管理員在授權名單管理；移除後，該員工編號立即無法登入。如需刪除授權名單或操作紀錄中與您有關的資料，請同樣寄信至上述信箱。

4. 自動刪除

  • 排程發布操作紀錄：90 天後自動刪除。
  • 待發留言：該貼文的留言全部完成、取消或失敗後 14 天自動刪除。
  • 粉專存取權杖快取：1 小時後自動失效。
  • 錯誤紀錄：30 天後自動刪除。
  • 瀏覽器中的偏好設定與登入憑證：清除瀏覽器資料即可立即刪除。

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 18, 2026

"Social Media Editor Toolbox" does not offer Facebook Login to the general public, so it does not store personal data of general Facebook users. For what data the Tool stores and for how long, see Section 3 of the Privacy Policy.

1. Remove the Tool's Facebook Access

A Facebook account that has authorized the Tool can remove that access at any time:

  1. Log in to Facebook, click your profile picture (top right) → "Settings & privacy" → "Settings".
  2. Open "Apps and Websites" (or "Business Integrations").
  3. Find this Tool and click "Remove".

Once removed, the access token stored by the Tool becomes invalid immediately and can no longer post on your or your Page's behalf.

2. Request Deletion of Data Stored by the Tool

To delete the access token, Page settings, comment templates, or pending comments stored by the Tool, email jeanzhuang@tvbs.com.tw with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your Facebook account name or Page name. We will delete the data and confirm by email within 30 days.

3. Team Member Access and Data

Team members' access to scheduling is managed by an administrator on the allowlist; once removed, that employee ID can no longer sign in immediately. To delete allowlist or activity-log data about you, email the same address above.

4. Automatic Deletion

  • Scheduling activity log: deleted after 90 days.
  • Pending comments: deleted 14 days after all comments for that post are completed, canceled, or failed.
  • Page access token cache: expires after 1 hour.
  • Error logs: deleted after 30 days.
  • Preferences and sign-in credential in the browser: deleted immediately by clearing browser data.
